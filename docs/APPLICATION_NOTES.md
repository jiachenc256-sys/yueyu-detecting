## How Speak stays usable (talking points)

1. **① Archive check** — lyrics / light fingerprints; scene vernacular + archive-linked linguistic note + speaker when labeled (not an LLM summary).
2. **② Delivery / prosody** — browser audio features for coarse emotion — not an emotion LLM; not a claim that lyrics were heard clearly.
3. **Honest CER** — dual metrics; no mean 0.5 claim (lab Jingchai adapted ≈ 0.81; Baitu unseen ≈ 1.04).
